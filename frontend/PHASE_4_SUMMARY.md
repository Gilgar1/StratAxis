## 📦 What Was Built

### 1. Smart Insights (`/insights`)
- **Feature**: AI-powered feed of market opportunities.
- **Visuals**: Color-coded cards (Green=Opportunity, Red=Risk) with confidence scores.
- **Value**: Transforms raw data into "Buy/Sell" signals.

### 2. Data Quality Dashboard (`/data-quality`)
- **Feature**: Transparency report on data sources.
- **Components**: Recharts Pie Chart for source distribution, progress bars for confidence metrics.
- **Trust**: Validates the "98% Refresh Rate" claim to users.

### 3. Comparison Tool (`/comparison`)
- **Feature**: Side-by-side battle card view.
- **Capacity**: Compare up to 3 neighborhoods instantly.
- **Metrics**: Aligns Price, Growth, Listings, and Volatility in a readable grid.

### 4. Scenario & Yield Calculator (`/scenario`)
- **Feature**: Interactive ROI estimator.
- **Inputs**: Sliders for Land Cost and Construction Budget.
- **Real-time**: Gross/Net Yield and Break-even years update instantly as you drag sliders.

### 5. Watchlists (`/watchlists`)
- **Feature**: Personal tracking dashboard.
- **Action**: Add high-potential zones to a "monitor" list.
- **Status**: Live 30-day percentage change indicators.

### 6. Alerts & Notifications (`/alerts`)
- **Feature**: Notification preference center.
- **Channels**: Email and In-App toggle.

### 7. Export & Reporting (`/export`)
- **Feature**: Download center for PDF Market Reports and CSV data.

### 8. Admin Panel (`/admin`)
- **Feature**: Backend management interface.
- **Capabilities**: User role management and System Health monitoring.

## 🚀 How to Demo

(Ensure you are logged in. Use `demo@strataxis.cm` / `demo123`)

1. **Insights**: `http://localhost:3000/insights`
   - Read the top "Opportunity" card.
2. **Calculator**: `http://localhost:3000/scenario`
   - Drag the "Land Acquisition Cost" slider and watch the ROI change.
3. **Comparison**: `http://localhost:3000/comparison`
   - Click "Add Makepe" to see it compared against Bonapriso.
4. **Data Quality**: `http://localhost:3000/data-quality`
   - Hover over the chart to see source breakdown.
